# Injuries and Effects

## Damage attachments and resistance

Use Add Effects on supported damage cards, or the effect controls on area recipient rows. An effect's exposure, resistance and consequence can be separate from the weapon's ordinary HP damage. Resistance must beat the DV; ties fail.

| Effect | Resolution |
| --- | --- |
| Poison | Resist Torture/Drugs DV13; failure offers 2d6 direct HP damage without armor ablation. |
| Biotoxin | Resist Torture/Drugs DV15; failure offers 3d6 direct HP damage without armor ablation. |
| EMP / Microwaver | Cybertech DV15; failure opens the supported two-item disablement workflow. |
| Flashbang | Resist Torture/Drugs DV15; temporary Damaged Eye and Damaged Ear, no injury bonus damage. |
| Teargas | Resist Torture/Drugs DV13; temporary Damaged Eye. |
| Sleep | Resist Torture/Drugs DV13; Sleep/Unconscious plus Prone and wake handling. |
| On Fire (Mild), from Incendiary | Supported native incendiary handling requires qualifying armor penetration. |
| Smoke | Separate saved scene footprint and lifetime; no damage or resistance roll. |

Biological eligibility and immunity are GM rulings. Ordinary native ammunition damage is not reapplied just because an exposure is reported. Armor-Piercing and Smart are attack/damage behaviors, not actor statuses.

## Critical injuries and reminders

Native injury items retain their own modifiers. Combat Tools adds supported Evasion guards, MOVE-floor handling, aimed-head Cracked Skull correction and advisory injury warnings. It does not replace every native injury mechanic.

More than 4 m/yd on foot can create separate Broken Ribs, Foreign Object (Body) and Foreign Object (Head) cards. **Apply 5 damage** is explicit, normally at the proper turn-end timing. Resetting movement below the threshold or removing the injury can withdraw pending eligibility; this does not undo damage already paid.

Spinal Injury and ear-related next-turn Action warnings are advisory. General Action spending remains manual.

## Burning and waking

Highest active native fire severity ticks once at the affected turn end. There is no automatic out-of-combat fire ticking. Extinguish removes actor-owned fire effects or disables relevant item-owned ones. All On Fire severities also clear when the participant's combat ends, resets or is deleted, including manually applied fire. Cleanup does not apply another damage tick and preserves fire belonging to another started encounter.

Module-owned Sleep can expire or wake on damage/touch while preserving unrelated unconsciousness. **Prone remains after waking.** Current source also provides **Wake using action** from another token's HUD for a conscious owned helper and an unconscious target. Touching range and Action cost are adjudicated by players/GM.

## Duration and cleanup

One minute is 20 rounds at the relevant turn index in started combat, or game-time seconds outside combat. Paused real-world time is not itself a game-time advance.

Encounter-owned temporary sensory injury items/markers and applicable temporary effects clean up on expiry or encounter end/reset/deletion. Permanent injuries, preexisting conditions and other encounters' effects are preserved. Newly activated damage conditions can carry encounter cleanup even without a timer. Critical injuries and Dead are not assigned this general encounter-end cleanup.

Supported drug expiry disables effects while retaining inventory. Smoke and EMP have their own saved lifetimes and cleanup. Native damage undo does not roll back every secondary effect. Historical instant-effect cards may still show Wake/Extinguish controls after external cleanup; inspect current actor state.
