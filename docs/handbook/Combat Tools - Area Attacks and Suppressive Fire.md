# Area Attacks and Suppressive Fire

## Grenades, rockets and shotgun shells

1. Choose the supported attack from the target or self-HUD menu. Self-HUD grenades can begin placement without a separate target.
2. Aim the area. Left-click accepts placement; Escape/right-click cancels the preview.
3. The initial aim square must have sight-wall line of sight from the attacker. Invalid squares cannot be placed; sight is rechecked before ammo consumption.
4. Complete the native attack. On a missed explosive attack, the GM places the actual landing point using the card's reposition control. Scatter is manual; qualifying Smart ammunition may offer its native retry first.
5. Review the affected tokens. Each recipient resolves its own defense and any required relocation.
6. Roll shared damage once and apply it separately to each intended recipient. Complete individual resistance/effect controls.

Recipient geometry is a saved snapshot using wall-clipped coverage and token footprints. The GM can add/exclude recipients or override affected outcomes. **The GM resolves all aspects of cover and terrain**, including destruction and damage to cover.

In a tracked encounter, source and required recipients must belong to that originating encounter. An added target cannot silently come from another encounter.

## Shape and defense settings

Explosives support square or circle profiles. Shells and suppressive fire have their own configurable geometry. Native ammunition distinguishes shells from slugs; shotgun-shell attack DV is 13.

Evasion, optional Cover Up and MOVE costs follow campaign settings. Avoidance and relocation are separate from HP application. Attack results/dice wait for the necessary placement and responses to complete.

## Suppressive fire

Use the native ten-round Autofire attack. Each affected target makes its own Concentration response; a tie resists. Failure records the obligation to seek cover. Players and GM adjudicate the Move/Run, Action expenditure and suitable cover.

The current default profile is a three-square ray. A RAW radius/line-of-sight option is available; do not infer the geometry from the word “suppression” alone.

## Attack markers and smoke

The GM's Show/Hide attack-area control affects the attack marker. Completion hides it after the required steps; deleting the card removes its attack marker. After manual scatter, the original aim marker identifies the original target square.

Smoke is separate: it creates a saved footprint with its own display and lifetime, survives attack-marker hiding and chat deletion, and can persist through reload. Active smoke crossing the attacker's center-to-target line adds the native **−4 obscured-task modifier once**; blast attacks check the chosen impact point. The native dialog offers **Ignore smoke** for equipment or a GM ruling. No automatic inference of vision equipment or vertical smoke volume is made.

**Current working-tree behavior:** GM Remove smoke / Restore smoke toggles both visibility and automatic obscuration. The original lifetime continues while removed. Expired or deleted smoke cannot be restored.
