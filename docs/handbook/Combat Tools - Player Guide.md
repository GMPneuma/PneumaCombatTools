# Player Guide

## Before play

1. Set your assigned **Character** in Foundry's player configuration.
2. Select one token you own. Right-click another token to open target actions; right-click your own token for self controls. Owned-target routing can depend on your Token HUD preference.
3. Keep the character sheet available for inventory, normal skills and character management.
4. Customize Combat Bar, Biomonitor and Token HUD under **Configure Settings → Module Settings → Pneuma's Combat Tools**.

## Make an attack

1. Select your acting token and right-click the target.
2. Choose the weapon and attack mode. Complete the native CPR roll dialog.
3. Wait for the defender's choice when a response is offered. The native attack has already rolled; its visible result is held until defense resolves.
4. Read the combined outcome. Roll damage from the card; Shift-click opens native damage options.
5. Check the intended recipient and attached effects, then apply damage. **To selected target** deliberately redirects an application; check your selection first.
6. Resolve any separate resistance or injury controls. Inspect the application receipt before doing anything twice.

Empty guns expose Reload and Change Ammo. On loaded guns, right-click the weapon name, or use Shift+F10, to switch between attack and ammunition controls. Bows and crossbows have a separate **Load & Continue** prompt when empty.

## Respond to an attack

- Open the incoming chat card from the notification, or directly in chat.
- Choose **Evade** or **Don't Evade** when offered. Evasion availability follows the GM's rules and your current conditions.
- For an area effect, respond on your own row and complete any requested relocation outside the area.
- **Cover Up**, if enabled, is a no-roll homebrew alternative: remain in place, become Prone and accept its armor/ablation consequences.
- Dismissing a HUD notification does not answer the attack.

## Other actions

| Situation | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Grapple a target | Target HUD → Close Combat → Grab; confirm the free hand and roll Brawling. |
| Already grappling | Use your self-HUD Close Combat controls for Choke, Throw, Release or Escape. |
| Use a grenade | Open the self-HUD thrown/grenade menu, choose it and place the area. |
| Netrun against a character | Jack In, then use QuickHack while the tracked connection is active. |
| Detected incoming netrunner | Right-click the Neural Intrusion icon for the appropriate Eject choice. |
| On fire | Right-click the On Fire icon for Extinguish; account for the required Action at the table. |
| Help an unconscious character | With a conscious owned helper selected, use the target's **Wake using action** control when available; GM adjudicates touching range and Action cost. |
| Make an ad-hoc roll | Click the die beside chat's roll-mode selector. |
| Finish your turn | Use End Turn when you own the active participant and a GM is connected. |

## Movement and damage reminders

The movement counter shows allowance usage, not an automatic Action budget. Reset restores the saved start position/elevation and tracked movement; it does not undo attacks or HP changes. Broken Ribs and Foreign Object can create **Apply 5 damage** cards after more than 4 m/yd on foot. Apply the appropriate damage at the proper time; a warning is not automatic payment.

If a card becomes unavailable after an encounter changes, ask the GM to inspect it. Avoid repeatedly clicking damage on an uncertain application.
