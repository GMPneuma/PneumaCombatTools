# Grappling

## Establish a grapple

1. Select the acting token and open the nearby target's Close Combat menu.
2. Choose Grab and confirm a free hand.
3. Roll native Brawling. The defender responds with Brawling from the card; ties favor the responder.
4. On success, choose **Hold Target** or **Take Held Object**. Physical inventory transfer for a held object remains manual.

Managed grapple penalties apply without duplicating an existing native modifier. Inappropriate two-handed attack entries are blocked. A held token follows the grappler's previous position/elevation and uses a reduced texture scale; ending the grapple restores its saved scale. Held-defender player movement is blocked, but GM corrections remain possible.

## Continue or break the hold

| Actor | Control | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grappler | Self HUD → Choke | Applies current prepared BODY directly to HP; tracks successive choking rounds. |
| Grappler | Self HUD → Throw | Applies BODY directly to HP, makes target Prone and ends hold. |
| Grappler | Self HUD → Release | Ends hold without undoing previous damage. |
| Defender | Self HUD → Escape | New opposed contest; hold persists on failure. |
| Third party | Target grappler → Break Grapple | New opposed contest; hold persists on failure. |
| GM | End/recovery controls | Removes the managed relationship and its linked consequences. |

Choke and Throw bypass armor and do not ablate it. In combat, attacker follow-ups wait until after the establishing turn; Choke cannot apply twice in one round. Actions and getting up remain table-managed.

Three successive choking rounds can cause unconsciousness independently of remaining HP. The source's HP safeguard is narrow: it leaves 1 HP when starting HP is greater than 1 and the result would be below 0; an exact 0 result does not enter that branch. Do not assume it universally prevents reaching 0 HP.

## Outside combat

Grapples can be scene-scoped outside a started encounter. Timing is then managed at the table. Starting combat afterward does not automatically adopt that scene grapple into combat timing. Resolve or re-establish the relationship deliberately.
