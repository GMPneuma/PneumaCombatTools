# Evasion and Homebrew

## Ranged evasion modes

| Mode | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| RAW | Current REF 8+ or installed functional Reflex Co-Processor qualifies. Evasion uses DEX + Evasion. No added per-round allowance, repeated-evasion penalty or mandatory LUCK fee. |
| No Ranged Evasion | Removes ranged-evasion choices from Combat Tools attacks. |
| Homebrew | Uses configured qualifiers, free attempts and extra-attempt policy. Requires the participants in a started encounter. |

Awareness, injuries, cyberware disablement and attack-specific restrictions can still prevent a response. Eligibility and the roll's governing STAT are different concepts.

## Configure homebrew

The GM selects Homebrew beside Ranged Evasion and opens its configuration form. Main settings and the form save separately.

REF, Reflex Co-Processor and Solo Threat Detection can be configured to qualify, grant a free evasion, and allow that grant to stack. Non-stacking grants share one free allowance; stacking grants add individually. After free attempts, use the configured flat penalty, cumulative penalty, LUCK cost or no-additional-evasions rule. With no free grants, the extra-attempt policy starts on the first attempt.

The native dialog identifies applicable penalties and mandatory LUCK separately. Saved encounter accounting supports recovery without paying twice. These rules do not replace general Action adjudication.

## Area evasion and Cover Up

Area attacks have separate eligibility, penalty and MOVE-cost settings. Optional borrowing can carry movement debt. Successful avoidance can require selecting a legal destination outside the affected area.

**Cover Up** is a separate homebrew response, not an Evasion roll. It needs no evasion eligibility. The target stays in place, becomes Prone and takes the hit with doubled SP and doubled ablation, including blocked damage. It is not automatic physical-cover simulation.

## NPC automation

Automatic NPC evasion is off by default, applies only under RAW and excludes player-owned actors. Area automation also requires RAW eligibility without evasion penalties, movement costs or Cover Up. Changing those rules restores the decision workflow.
