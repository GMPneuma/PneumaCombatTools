# Pneuma's Combat Tools — 0.8.0

Combat workflows for Cyberpunk RED in Foundry VTT v12. Requires Cyberpunk RED Core, libWrapper and an active GM for coordinated actions.

## Major features

- **Attack and defense cards:** target-aware native attacks, Evade / Don't Evade, hidden attack results until responses, and combined outcomes.
- **Damage and critical injuries:** native damage application, three Add Effects slots, armor controls, critical-injury handling and visible application history.
- **Area attacks:** grenades, rockets, shotgun shells and suppressive fire, with placement, individual responses, GM scatter and shared damage.
- **Grappling:** grabs, holds, escapes, third-party breaks, choking and throws, with persistent self-HUD controls.
- **QuickHacks:** Jack-In, detection, hacking, Jack Out and ejection, with configurable availability and supported effect automation.
- **EMP and cyberware disablement:** configurable manual/random selection, limited player shortlists, eligibility and weighting, hardened cyberware and timed restoration.
- **Combat bar:** four dock/layout choices, large portraits, scrolling, native initiative controls, End Turn and GM movement modes.
- **Movement and injury support:** movement counters, reset/start markers, injury-damage cards and contextual reminders.
- **Biomonitor and alerts:** EKG/vitals, injuries, active drugs, disabled cyberware, Neural Intrusion and private messages.
- **Manual rolls:** damage, critical injury, Cyberpunk/custom dice, roll-under STAT checks and GM group skill requests.

## Smaller features and conveniences

- Shift-click the native armor-ablation arrow to restore one SP.
- Optional Martial Arts no-ablation rule and generic attack names to hide weapons.
- Clearer native attack modifiers and improvised-damage fields.
- GM combat-bar status flyouts and native hidden/defeated controls.

- Ranged DV hover includes elevation and optional Autofire.
- Unaware-defender option and optional RAW-only automatic NPC evasion.
- Configurable ranged-evasion homebrew, including free allowances, penalties and LUCK costs.
- Optional AoE MOVE costs and Cover Up homebrew.
- GM target-list corrections and attack-area Show/Hide controls.
- Shared AoE damage results collected beneath the roll.
- Poison, Biotoxin, Flashbang, Teargas, Sleep, Ignite, Smoke, EMP and Microwaver effect flows.
- Native fire ticking, Extinguish, Sleep wake-up and temporary-effect cleanup.
- Persistent smoke areas with their own expiry and GM removal.
- Interact With Armor and Half Armor SP, with rounded-up SP; always available for ad-hoc damage, optional elsewhere.
- Collapsible Add Effects categories without addictions or wounded-state clutter.
- Selected-target Applied to history on normal and ad-hoc damage cards.
- Broken Ribs and both Foreign Object injuries have separate damage reminders.
- Cracked Skull headshot correction, injury MOVE floor and sensory/action reminders.
- Self-HUD Escape names the grappler; follow-up grapple results preserve the original card.
- Multiple detected netrunners have individual Eject choices.
- Optional speedware initiative reroll and self-HUD thrown weapons/grenades.
- Item Used/Disabled badges preserve original item names.
- Combat bar portrait sizes from 32 to 96 pixels; name-only tooltips and minimize controls.
- Players list cycles through Online, All and Minimized.
- Player HUD follows the assigned character even without selecting a token.
- Left/right Biomonitor docking and optional Crew Tools HUD integration.
- Minimized EKG, hidden HP reveal on hover/focus and status-colored alert shortcut.
- Hover EKG for Medtechs, targets with Biomonitors, or everyone with the GM option.
- Six-second queued alerts; private HUD messaging API for macros and other modules.
- Group-check player buttons, optional hidden DV, colored results and expandable native roll details.
- Custom dice from 1d1 through 20d100; standard rolls retain native Critical Success/Failure behavior.
- Compact settings, keyboard-friendly menus and consistent chat buttons that do not shift on hover.

Combat Tools supports the table without tracking a general Action budget. Some QuickHack decisions, treatment, held-object transfers and situational rulings remain manual. Screen glitches are now separate in PneumaVisualTools; Combat Tools does not include a fire-screen overlay.
